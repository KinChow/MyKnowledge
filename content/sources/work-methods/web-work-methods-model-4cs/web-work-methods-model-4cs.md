Robert F. Lauterborn proposed a 4 Cs classification in 1990, a more consumer-orientated version of the 4 Ps, consisting of the 4 Cs: consumer, cost, convenience, and communication. The 4 Cs model provides a demand/customer co-creation alternative to the well-known 4 Ps supply side model of marketing management.
