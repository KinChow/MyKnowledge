A minimum viable product (MVP) is a version of a product with just enough features to be usable by early customers who can then provide feedback for future product development. The term was coined and defined in 2001 by Frank Robinson and then popularized by Steve Blank and Eric Ries. The MVP is analogous to experimentation in the scientific method applied in the context of validating business hypotheses.
