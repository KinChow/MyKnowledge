The Five Ws is a checklist used in journalism to ensure that the lead contains all the essential points of a story. The fundamental five questions are: Who? What? When? Where? Why? The Five Ws are also used to guide research and interviews, and are often extended with How, forming the 5W1H (and 5W2H) analysis framework.
