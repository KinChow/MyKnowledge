PDCA (plan–do–check–act), sometimes called plan–do–check–adjust, is an iterative design and management method used in business for the control and continual improvement of processes and products. It is also known as the Shewhart cycle, or the control circle/cycle. The cycle originated with physicist Walter Shewhart at the Bell Telephone Laboratories in the 1920s, and W. Edwards Deming modified it in the 1940s.
