The Ansoff matrix is a strategic planning tool that provides a framework to help executives, senior managers, and marketers devise strategies for future business growth. It is named after Russian American Igor Ansoff, an applied mathematician and business manager, who created the concept. He describes four growth alternatives for growing an organization in existing or new markets, with existing or new products: market penetration, market development, product development, and diversification.
