S.M.A.R.T. is a mnemonic acronym, giving criteria to guide in the setting of objectives and goals, for example in project management, employee performance management and personal development. In the November 1981 issue of Management Review, George T. Doran's paper introduced the S.M.A.R.T. criteria: Specific (targeting a particular area for improvement), Measurable (quantifying, or at least suggesting, an indicator of progress), Assignable (defining responsibility clearly), Realistic (outlining attainable results with available resources), and Time-related (including a timeline for expected results). The SMART acronym is linked to Peter Drucker's management by objectives (MBO) concept.
